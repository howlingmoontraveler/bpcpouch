Common questions

Is there nicotine in this?

No. Zero nicotine, zero tobacco. We use the oral pouch format because it's convenient and delivers right under the lip — the payload is strictly BPC-157.

What is BPC-157?

BPC-157 is a 15-amino-acid peptide heavily researched for tissue and gut support. It is not an FDA-approved drug.

How do I use a pouch?

Tuck one pouch between your upper lip and gum for 20–40 minutes, then toss it. No chewing, no mess, no spitting required.

Does it need to be refrigerated?

Nope. Just keep the tin closed in a cool, dry spot away from direct sun.

Who shouldn't use this?

Anyone under 21, or if you're pregnant or nursing. As always, check with your doctor if you have specific medical conditions.

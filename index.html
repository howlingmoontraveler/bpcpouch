Questions, answered plainly.

Is this nicotine?

No. BPouCh-157 contains no nicotine and no tobacco. The pouch format is borrowed from oral nicotine products because the form factor is already understood — the payload is a peptide.

What is BPC-157?

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide (pentadecapeptide) originally described as a fragment of a protein found in gastric juice. It is widely discussed in research and wellness circles for tissue and gut-related work. It is not an FDA-approved drug.

How do I use a pouch?

Place one pouch under the upper lip for 20–40 minutes. Do not chew it. Discard after use. Typical personal use is one pouch daily, or as directed by a qualified clinician.

Does it need to be refrigerated?

Store the sealed can in a cool, dry place away from direct sun. Do not leave it in a hot car. Once opened, keep the lid snapped shut.

Who should not use this?

Not for anyone under 21. Not for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding. If you have a medical condition or take prescription drugs, talk to a clinician first. This is not a substitute for medical care.
